## Conflict and repair

The shared quick-conflict control uses a durable request ID. A lost response can be retried without creating another conflict. The bounded queue accepts mainland conflicts and keeps Hawaii neutral. A quick conflict ends after 45 real-time-equivalent seconds of simulation. Repeated serious incidents can also provoke a funded, sufficiently aggressive actual national leader; ordinary trade lowers tension and peace imposes a seven-day escalation cooldown.

Only actual staffed and funded batteries fire. Unlicensed foreign helicopters can be intercepted, while flight permits protect legitimate aircraft. Projectiles use actual impact positions; misses can hit real buildings, cause fires and injure or kill nearby residents. No distant victims are invented. Civilian warnings route residents to real shelter, preserving committed transport and emergency response. Peace cancels unresolved combat. War-damaged buildings enter real reconstruction with the destination government's budget, material escrow/delivery, construction labour and inspection; ordinary owner-funded fire repair remains separate.
