# Shared metered electricity across the three countries

North City and Hawaii keep their existing physical household electricity meters. The Hansdrex plant supplies this connected network, so their consumption is an electricity import paid through the existing provider billing and arrears ledger. Country separation does not create a free North grid or remove its demand from Hansdrex generation.

Demand includes active connected households and unique physical commercial premises. National treasuries, escrow wallets and supplier bookkeeping without a physical address are not additional electricity customers. An operating enterprise at the same address as an existing business does not double that building's load. The plant reports national connected household and premises counts.

Grid recovery requires actual workers, money, fuel and completed overhauls. Each pair of physically present operators can commission one 100-unit generating module in an hourly work period. Independent crews work in parallel; costs scale with the actual purchased capacity, stay within the target and settle once per hour. A grid is stable only after maintenance reaches 90% and output covers at least 72% of load.
