# Map 29: stable vehicles, 1,000 residents, river districts and actual military operations

## Vehicle disappearance

Two reproducible faults affected personal cars and freight trucks: selection tested the old server position while drawing an interpolated position, and deleting an out-of-view motion checkpoint allowed the same old snapshot to recreate the vehicle repeatedly. Selection now uses the displayed position for continuing journeys. Dormant checkpoints retain identity/progress while offscreen, without running hidden animation or submitting GPU instances, and are removed when the authoritative actor disappears. The active car budget remains 120. Distance preference is an actual eight-metre retention band instead of subtracting 64 from squared distance. Focused tests cover both entering and leaving the view, stale plans, dense budgets, phase changes, resumption and removal.

## People and costs

A one-time migration reserves real operational homes before creating exactly the deficit to 1,000 living residents. It prioritizes at least 100 new residents each in North and West when the deficit permits, and places the requested military intake inside this total. Imported personal capital and furnishings are accounted for separately from domestic revenue. Existing identities, brains, wallets and deeds are retained. Population continues to change through ordinary births, deaths and immigration after this one-time intake.

West policy multipliers: wage .90, housing .25, property .22, food .38, living .42, pollution .42 and amenities .86. Asking prices, recurring rent/maintenance and utilities follow the revised policy; existing owners' equity is not reset. The lower costs and new river promenades improve affordability and amenities while retaining the industrial role.

The neural admission bound follows the shared population limit rather than rejecting shard inputs above 64. Per-alarm computation remains four checkpoints and the coordinator retains 16 shards with population-adjusted cadence. Entire living shard memberships remain intact so cleanup cannot erase another chunk's checkpoints.

## Geography

A 20-metre western channel uses the former north/south ring-road corridor at x=-430. A 36-metre northern river at z=-570 separates North City from the airfield and Main City, meeting the existing eastern river. Ordinary bridge crossings have separate sidewalks and axis-correct rails; motorway and metro crossings remain connected. The airport stays on the Main City bank. All 654 prior buildings preserve their IDs and geometry. Water rectangles are unioned before rendering, avoiding overlapping planes. A one-time migration re-banks stranded ground actors/parked cars and replans affected journeys without resetting their finances or transport commitments.

## Military

Fifty actual residents serve in the army, including an experienced commander, officers and enlisted ranks. Newly authorized cadets have real homes and are reserved before other public recruitment can take their positions. Existing essential workers are retained. A parade ground, firing range, aircraft, naval command building, jetty and two endowed patrol vessels use authoritative footprints. Ship and crew motion share a pure trajectory/boarding helper between the viewer and simulation. Equipment accents follow actual visible bodies, not duplicate soldiers. Seven canonical 3.2-metre perimeter wall segments enclose the barracks and airfield with a main gate and two naval gate openings. The same geometry blocks ground navigation, cars and walking players; gate routes and complete aircraft circuits have clearance. The wall adds three merged static material batches and no lights or per-frame work. Four gate posts are staffed from twelve existing enlisted residents over three eight-hour shifts. Commanders, officers and qualified pilots are retained for their roles. Guards must walk to their post before earning duty pay; training and naval selection exclude these assignments, and off-duty guards return to quarters. No additional guard NPCs are spawned.

Daily phases, attendance, pilot qualification, weather, treasury-funded fuel/payroll and actual crew boarding govern exercises. Cadets physically return to barracks quarters for rest and a finite mess supply purchased from existing food businesses; their civilian homes and deeds remain intact. Local base duties use pedestrian routes rather than choosing a private-car or metro detour. Sampled background movement consumes the elapsed movement budget across checked route segments, with a 64-waypoint bound; velocity remains in ordinary per-second units. Rifles, initial ammunition and vessels have an explicit one-time external equipment-endowment ledger. Further ammunition consumes public funds. Abstract game shots expend rounds, stop at solid walls, and can miss, injure or kill actual people in their path; casualty recording is idempotent. Onsite practice improves weapon skill and safety. Safety experience and incident reflection reduce aim error; empathy and learned caution can cause a shooter to hold fire when an actual friendly body is in the firing path, without spending ammunition. Incidents affect discipline, memory and stress. This is a bounded simulation of choices, not a claim of human self-awareness. No real-world weapon specifications or tactics are modeled.

## Atmosphere

Clear conditions have no distance fog before 1,600 metres and fade only at 6,500 metres; rain gently shortens this to 950–4,300 metres. One shared range helper prevents time-of-day and photo restoration paths from reinstating dense fog. Sunrise/sunset colors and the weather controls retain their behavior.

## Verification

A local replay of the real map-28 checkpoint preserved all 3,023 archived/resident records, brains, wallets and homes, added 286 residents (118 North, 119 West, 49 Europe), reached 1,000 living residents and 50 soldiers with two ships, and advanced 600 game seconds without new simulation faults. Production has natural ongoing population changes, so the precise new intake is calculated again from the live saved checkpoint during deployment. No production test-state import or reset is part of this release.

The expanded city/neural run exercised 332 cases. An ordinary-step metro waypoint regression found during the elapsed-movement change was fixed and its complete staircase/coarse-movement group rerun successfully; the other 331 city/neural cases passed. All 203 existing viewer unit tests passed, as did focused physical-wall, gate-routing, aircraft-clearance, merged-rendering and atmosphere checks. Production build and diff checks passed. Scaling fixtures now supply a real vacant European rental block and allow a full operating day for paid grid expansion to meet the larger population, rather than assuming the old 728-person demand/vacancies. The airfield, apron and passenger terminal are explicitly tested as Main City.

The two-day real-checkpoint replay with the fence and guard rota completed six training flights, two naval patrols and twelve range rounds, with no firearm casualties or friendly hits. Four guards were physically present at their posts in the overnight samples. It exposed a separate legacy private-hiring rule that silently recruited low-income soldiers away from service; ordinary startup/hiring now excludes active public-service and essential facility commitments. A real enterprise simulation regression verifies civilian hiring still succeeds while military and essential jobs remain intact. Ground drills no longer grant aviation skill; only actual flight time does.
